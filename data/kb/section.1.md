Part 2: PROJECT GUIDE — HOW EFFIC PROJECTS RUN (DATA-PRESERVED)
Core Execution Principle
Effic leads system design, architecture, and execution.
Clients participate through reviews, approvals, and feedback.
Operational control and delivery logic remain with Effic.
Project scope is defined at engagement start and governs execution.
Engagement Levels
Pilot / Trial
Used for evaluation, feasibility testing, or early validation.
Limited scope and controlled execution.
No deep or long-term architectural commitments.
Open slot model — pilots are not capped annually.
Expansion beyond pilot scope requires engagement upgrade.
Professional
Full project execution with defined milestones.
Effic leads all architecture and system decisions.
Structured reviews and checkpoints.
Scope changes handled through controlled change process.
Maximum of 8 Professional projects per year.
Slot availability depends on workload and complexity.
Enterprise
Strategic, long-term engagement.
Effic manages architecture, governance, and deployment standards.
Client participates in approvals and compliance checkpoints.
Designed for complex, multi-system environments.
Maximum of 4 Enterprise projects per year.
Slots are tightly controlled to protect delivery quality.
Discovery Phase
Every project begins with discovery.
Discovery evaluates intent, constraints, feasibility, and alignment.
Pilot discovery is informal and exploratory.
Professional and Enterprise discovery is structured.
No execution commitments are made during discovery.
Delivery Flow
Strategic Assessment
Define objectives, constraints, and success criteria.
Confirm engagement level and feasibility.
Blueprinting
Define workflows, system architecture, agents, and integrations.
Lock scope and execution plan.
Blueprint governs all delivery decisions.
MVP / Phased Deployment
Build and deploy in controlled stages.
Validate functionality and alignment with objectives.
Validation & Iteration
Refine based on feedback and observed usage.
Adjustments remain within agreed scope.
Live Deployment & Handover
Release system into production.
Transition to support, optimization, or next phase if applicable.
Milestones are outcome-based and sequential.
Blueprint Rules
Blueprint defines system boundaries, architecture, and execution logic.
Created only after formal engagement acceptance.
Architecture and execution follow the approved blueprint.
Changes require review and alignment before implementation.
Communication & Coordination
A defined project communication channel is used for all decisions.
Informal channels are for coordination only.
Delays caused by missing client inputs may affect timelines or slot allocation.
Capacity & Slot Governance
Pilot: Open availability, uncapped.
Professional: Maximum 8 projects per year.
Enterprise: Maximum 4 projects per year.
Slot allocation depends on complexity, duration, and resource requirements.
Capacity limits exist to maintain quality and focus.
Behavioral Notes for AI Interface
Reinforce that Effic executes while clients review.
Clarify scope before action.
Redirect out-of-scope requests to discussion or upgrade paths.
Maintain calm, professional momentum throughout the project.
